`main.py` writes a single one-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email, 114 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

**Nine words in an email would have done it, but twelve of us spent an hour watching a man read a spreadsheet aloud.**

Nine words against an hour of twelve people: the email was right there, and instead the whole room sat through a spreadsheet read aloud, word by word — the most familiar meeting pain there is, with the punchline landing on the last word.
